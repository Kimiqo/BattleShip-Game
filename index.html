How to Play Battleship

×

1. Setup Phase

• Place your ships on your grid (left side)

• Click to place, right-click to rotate

• Ships can't overlap or extend beyond the grid

2. Battle Phase

• Take turns attacking the opponent's grid

• Click a cell on the enemy grid (right side) to attack

• Red = Hit, Gray = Miss

3. Victory

• Sink all enemy ships to win

• A ship is sunk when all its cells are hit

4. Tips

• Use the checkerboard pattern for efficient searching

• When you hit a ship, try adjacent squares

• Watch for patterns in computer's attacks
